# MLX — Brand Kit

**Tagline:** Turning Intelligence into Infrastructure Value.

## Positioning
MLX is an **IP Commercialisation House** — not a generic AI company. It commercialises
AI and deep-tech intellectual property for the energy and infrastructure sectors,
bridging technology trapped in labs and patents and the real economy.

## Audience
1. **Technology / IP owners** — researchers, founders, institutions with valuable but stranded IP.
2. **Industry** — infrastructure operators, utilities, corporates and investors with operational,
   financial or risk problems technology could solve.

## Personality
Investment-grade, credible, technical but not hype-y. Systemic and repeatable — a
commercialisation *engine*, not a one-off consulting project. Serious infrastructure tone.

## Visual identity (Step 0 · Option B — proposed direction)
- **Base:** near-black `#0A0D10`
- **Panels:** graphite `#11161B` / `#161C22`
- **Lines:** white at 8% alpha
- **Accent (signal):** amber `#E8A23C` — used ONLY for CTAs, funnel highlights, status tags, glows
- **Text:** `#E8ECEF` · **Muted:** `#8A949E`
- **Headings:** Space Grotesk 500/700 (vendored)
- **Body:** Inter 400/500 (vendored)
- **Mono / labels / data:** JetBrains Mono 400 (vendored)
- **Wordmark:** "MLX" wordmark; optional abstract mark evoking bridge/funnel/node —
  no AI-cliché icons (brains, decorative circuits).

## The Engine (core model)
Source → Assess → Position → Validate → Structure → Scale.

## Focus sectors
Water & Dams · Renewable Energy · Energy Infrastructure · Industrial Infrastructure.
Common thread: better intelligence materially changes cost, risk, availability or operational performance.

## Flagship case study
**Predict — Water Intelligence.** AI-powered water intelligence for critical infrastructure
(dam/reservoir prediction and hazard monitoring). Status: POC → validation → licensing. Currently commercialising.

## Why MLX
Technology · Commercial · Access.

## Two routes
- "Have technology. Need a market?" → CTA: Submit a Technology
- "Have an infrastructure problem?" → CTA: Tell Us Your Challenge
